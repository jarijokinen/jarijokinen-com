This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you visit this
website.

## Information you provide

This website does not require you to create an account and does not provide
forms, comments, newsletters or other features through which personal
information is directly collected.

## Analytics

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by
Google, to understand how visitors use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

- pages visited and interactions with the website;
- approximate geographic location;
- browser, device, and operating system information;
- session and visit information; and
- identifiers stored in analytics cookies.

This information is used to understand website traffic and usage and to help
improve the website.

Analytics cookies are not used unless you have given your consent. You can
change or withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie settings on this
website.

For more information about how Google collects and processes data, see Google's
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) page.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see the [Google Privacy
Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

For more information about the cookies used by this website, see the 
[Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## Legal basis

Where consent is required, analytics data is processed on the basis of your
consent.

You can withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie settings.
Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before consent
was withdrawn.

## Data sharing and processing

Analytics data is processed by Google in connection with the Google Analytics
service.

This website does not sell your personal information.

Google may process data in accordance with its own terms and privacy policies
and may process data outside your country of residence.

## Data retention

Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings configured for
this website's Google Analytics property.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data.
Under applicable data protection law, these may include the right to:

- request access to your personal data;
- request correction or deletion of your personal data;
- request restriction of processing;
- object to certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Some of these rights may not apply in every situation or may be limited where
the website operator cannot identify you from the information available.
