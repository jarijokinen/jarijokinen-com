## About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the website uses cookies and
similar technologies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit
a website. They can be used to remember your preferences, enable website
functionality and collect information about how a website is used.

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function, while others, such as
analytics cookies, are used only with your consent.

## How we use cookies

This website uses necessary cookies to remember your cookie consent
preferences.

With your consent, this website also uses analytics cookies to understand how
visitors use the site. The information collected helps us understand website
traffic and usage and improve the website.

Analytics cookies are not set unless you give your consent.

## Cookies we use

| Name | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `cf_country` | Jari Jokinen | Stores the visitor's country to determine whether the cookie consent banner should be displayed. |
| `consent_*` | Jari Jokinen | Stores the user's consent state. |
| `_ga` | Google | Creates a unique ID used to generate statistical data about how the visitor uses the site. |
| `_ga_#` | Google | Collects data on the number of times a visitor has visited the site, as well as the dates of their first and most recent visits. |

## Analytics

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics to collect information
about how visitors use the website. This may include information such as pages
visited, interactions with the website, approximate location derived from
technical information, device and browser information, and the duration of
visits.

Google may process information collected through Google Analytics in accordance
with its own privacy policies.

Analytics cookies are used only after you have given your consent.

## Managing your cookie preferences

When you first visit the website, you can choose whether to allow non-essential
cookies.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie settings
link available at the footer of this website. Withdrawing consent does not
affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before consent was
withdrawn.

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Please note
that blocking necessary cookies may affect some website functionality.

## Changes to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time, for example, when the
cookies or services used by the website change.

The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
